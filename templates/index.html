🛡️ Quick Security Tips
🔒
Check for HTTPS
Always verify the URL starts with https:// before entering sensitive data. Look for the padlock icon.
🔍
Examine the Domain
Carefully read the domain name. Attackers use typos like "paypa1.com" or "g00gle.com" to deceive.
📧
Be Email Wary
Do not click links in unsolicited emails. Go directly to websites by typing the address manually.
🔑
Enable 2FA
Two-factor authentication protects your accounts even if your password is compromised.
🚫
Trust HTTPS Wisely
HTTPS only means the connection is encrypted — it does NOT guarantee the site is legitimate.
📱
Hover Before Click
On desktop, hover over links to preview the actual URL in your browser's status bar before clicking.
