📚 CNS Concept: Audit Logging

In cybersecurity, audit logging is the practice of recording security-relevant events for review, forensic analysis, and compliance. This history page demonstrates how security systems maintain audit trails. Organizations use logs to detect patterns, investigate incidents, and prove regulatory compliance. Every URL analyzed here is stored with timestamp information using SQLite — a lightweight relational database.
